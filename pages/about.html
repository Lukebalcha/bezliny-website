How It Works

Non-Contact Cleaning

Our drone uses filtered water, heat, and moderate pressure — free from chemicals or mechanical abrasion. The Bezliny Drone Cleaner delivers effective cleaning without damaging surfaces.

Privacy & Data

Data Protection

The drones used during cleaning operations are controlled solely by the pilot, and the recording function is completely disabled — no videos or images are captured or stored.

For certain projects, an additional small drone may be used to film the cleaning drone in action, strictly for marketing or documentation, and only with the client's prior consent.

We are dedicated to upholding the highest levels of privacy and data security.
