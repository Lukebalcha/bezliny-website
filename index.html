Privacy & Data Protection

We are committed to upholding the highest standards of privacy and data protection. The drones used during cleaning operations are controlled solely by the pilot, and the recording function is completely disabled — no videos or images are captured or stored.

For certain projects, an additional small drone may be used to film the cleaning drone in action strictly for marketing purposes or documentation, and only with the client's prior consent.
