Cleaning, Surface Treatment, and Inspection in Hazardous Environments

At Bezliny, our drone-based cleaning, surface treatment, and inspection services eliminate the need for workers to operate in hazardous environments.

This dramatically reduces—or even removes—the cost and complexity of scaffolding and rope access. We specialize in industrial and offshore cleaning, surface treatment, and inspection, providing sustainable and cost-effective solutions tailored for sectors like oil and gas.

These facilities are mainly composed of metallic structures that require careful cleaning to preserve protective coatings and extend their lifespan.

Our in-house developed Bezliny Drone Cleaner delivers highly effective cleaning without damaging surfaces, ensuring the long-term protection of critical assets.
